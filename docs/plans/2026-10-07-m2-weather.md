# M2 "Weather" implementation plan

> **For Claude:** implement this plan task by task, the way M1 was built: each task goes to a fresh
> subagent with the full task text, then a spec-compliance review, then a code-quality review; fixes
> are re-reviewed. Independent tasks run in parallel in git worktrees and are merged into `m2`.

**Goal:** the two strata M1 left out, and the delay between them and Space. **Air** plays sparse,
generative glass, bowls, bells, plucks and felt over the harmony; **Weather** plays a cloud of grains
over procedural fields, your WAVs, or the instrument's own **Memory** of the last 16 s; **Echo** is a
tape delay between the strata and Space. Their pages, eight new presets, and a worst case that stays
inside the 15% p99 gate.

**Architecture:** four new pieces in `dsp/`, each with the M1 strata's contract (a `*Patch` of real
values, `set()`, `reset()`, `seed()`, a `render()` that adds its dry into the buses and its Space send
into the send bus, no allocation after the constructor), joined to the engine the way Ground and Bloom
are. Echo is EffectForce's Delay, ported, as a send / return beside Space. Weather's grains are
EffectForce's Grain voices, ported, reading a fixed source instead of a live recording. The sources
come from a loader thread (PolyForce's `Loader`, ported) on the plugin side: procedural fields are
rendered there, WAVs are read there, and Memory's Keep writes its WAV there. The audio thread only
ever reads a published `const GrainSource*`.

**Tech stack:** C++17, GNU make on Linux (`g++` 13 for the x86 tests and ASan;
`arm-linux-gnueabihf-g++` 13 and `qemu-arm` for ARM), Python 3 with Pillow for the skin. New for the
budget: a `qemu-arm` with TCG plugins and the `insn` plugin (Task 1), so every task can count its
ARM instructions per block.

**Source documents:** [`docs/CONCEPT.md`](../CONCEPT.md) §5.3 (Air), §5.4 (Weather and Memory), §8
(Echo), §9 (pages), §10 (fields), §11 (budget), §13 (the `air` and `soak` tests);
[`docs/HANDOVER.md`](../HANDOVER.md); the M1 plan's

## The budget

The gate is p99 ≤ 15% of the 2902 µs block on the device, everything on at its heaviest. M1's worst case
measured **10.63%** p99 on the Force. Counted by `make arm-icount` (Task 1), that case is **396k** ARM
instructions a block on average, **413k** in its re-strike blocks (the counts this plan was first
written against, 423k and 433k, came from another way of counting; Task 1 re-baselined them). So a
thousand instructions is about **0.026–0.027 points of p99** (10.63 / 413k = 0.0257, / 396k = 0.0268);
the budget takes the dearer, **0.0268**. That leaves 4.4 points; M3 still has Patina (0.5, CONCEPT §11)
and the motion sources, matrix and scenes to come, so M2 plans for about 3.4 and keeps about 1 for M3.

## Decisions taken in this plan

These go into ROADMAP's decisions with Task 13.

1. **Air, Weather and Echo are off in Init** (levels and sends at 0): Init and the 0.0.2 presets play
   bit for bit as before, and the level contract holds without re-matching them. The new presets
   show the new strata.
2. **Echo sends are taken from each stratum's dry** (after its level, before its pan), by the engine:
   Ground's and Bloom's `render()` stay as they are. Bloom's tail handoff isn't echoed (its send
   boost is Space's).
3. **Weather reads only fixed sources:** a field, a WAV, or Memory's *remembered* 16 s, never the
   ring being recorded, so nothing Weather plays can feed itself back in real time. Remember swaps
   two rings (no copy on the audio thread).
4. **Sources are 16-bit stereo** with EffectForce's three levels (full, half and quarter rate),
   normalised to −20 dBFS RMS, and loop seamlessly (a crossfade at load). The grains convert as they
   read.
5. **Every source's root is C** for To Key: fields are tuned to C; a WAV's own pitch isn't detected
   in M2 (lifetime import, M3, brings pitch detection).
6. **Eight fields in M2** (Rain on Roof, Light Rain, Wind High, Wind Low, Surf, Stream, Embers,
   Night); the other four of CONCEPT §10 come with M4. Rendered on the loader thread on demand, not
   at load like the tables.
7. **STRATA's pages go stratum first:** GROUND, BLOOM, AIR, WEATHER, then DRONE, BLOOM OSC, AIR LOOP,
   GRAINS, so a tap on the tab moves your hands from one stratum to the next (CONCEPT §9's shared
   layout). The shared first bank's slot 6 is now each stratum's Echo send, as CONCEPT §9 planned.
8. **PLAY's bottom row is the four levels and the harmony with Air Density** (CONCEPT §9's bank 1):
   Space Level, Bloom Swell and Gravity leave PLAY for their own pages.
9. **Remember ignores a press within 2 s of the last** (a Q-Link turned through it would otherwise
   remember twice in a row) and while Keep copies the ring (milliseconds: Keep writes its file from the
   copy, so a Remember during the write, which on the SSD takes a second or two, is allowed and changes
   nothing in it; amended in the fix round, from "while Keep is writing"). The 2 s are counted in samples
   recorded since the last Remember (Task 6, as built): a fresh Memory isn't held back, and reset()
   restarts neither the count nor the generation.
10. **Memory survives reset() and the guard**; only a new Remember replaces what is remembered. The
    ring being recorded starts afresh at the guard and at CC 120 (`reset()`) only: Stop (the end of its
    Fade, Cut) and a long suspend leave the last 16 s recorded, so a Remember after a Stop finds what
    was played. *(The author's decision in Task 8's review; before it every Stop emptied the ring.)*

## Task 1: Instruction counts

**Goal:** `make arm-icount` prints each bench case's ARM instructions per 128-sample block, exactly
and repeatably, so every later task can measure its DSP against its share.

**Files:** modify `tools/bench.cpp` (an `--icount` mode), the `Makefile`, `docs/BUILDING.md`,
`docs/PERFORMANCE.md` ("How it is measured").

**Behaviour:**
- `afbench <so> --icount <case> <blocks>` sets the case up as the bench does (fresh instance, its
  parameters by index, 2 s untimed), waits for the tables, then renders `<blocks>` more blocks
  untimed and exits without printing timings.
- `make arm-icount` (variables `ICOUNT_QEMU`, default `/home/user/qemu-icount/qemu-arm-plugins`,
  and `ICOUNT_PLUGIN`, default `/home/user/qemu-icount/libinsn.so`) runs every case twice under
  `$(ICOUNT_QEMU) -L /usr/arm-linux-gnueabihf -plugin $(ICOUNT_PLUGIN) -d plugin` on the plain ARM
  build (not the profile-guided one: the profile is trained on the old code), at 256 and 768 blocks,
  and prints (count(768) − count(256)) / 512 per case, in thousands. Everything before the timed
  blocks (the table builder, the wait) is the same in both runs and cancels, provided the wait is
  deterministic: in `--icount` mode it joins the builder (polling `/proc` with a sleep would make the
  count depend on the machine).
- It says where the qemu binary was built from, and fails with a clear message if it is missing.

**Checks:**
1. Two runs of `make arm-icount` print the same numbers.
2. The M1 cases land where PERFORMANCE.md's instruction counts have them (idle ~1k, init chord ~210k,
   drone ~175k, bloom 6x2 ~355k, worst ~423k), within 5%; if not, say why in the commit (the counts
   in PERFORMANCE.md came from another qemu build) and record the new baseline in PERFORMANCE.md.

**Commit:** "Instruction counts: make arm-icount, each bench case's ARM instructions a block".

## Task 2: Echo, the delay

**Files:**
- **Copy from EffectForce, `ef` → `af`, with provenance lines:** `dsp/delay.h`, `dsp/delay.cpp`
  (7cf6e95). Its tests (`test/delay_test.cpp`, 3ae8a4d) become `test/echo_test.cpp`, trimmed to the
  checks of the Delay class itself.
- **Create:** `dsp/echo.h`, `dsp/echo.cpp`, `test/echo_test.cpp` (`void echoTests()`, called from
  `plugin_test.cpp`'s `main` after `reverbTests()`; `make test-module M=echo` works).

**Delay changes:** one new parameter, everything else bit for bit as EffectForce has it (its ported
checks pass unchanged):
- `float diffuse = 0.0f;  // 0..1: the repeats smeared, more with each pass`. Four Schroeder
  allpasses per side inside the loop, after the cuts and before the drive, delays 7.3, 11.9, 17.1
  and 23.7 ms (L) and 8.1, 12.7, 18.3 and 24.9 ms (R), coefficient 0.65 × diffuse each. At diffuse 0
  they are skipped entirely: bit for bit EffectForce's Delay. A diffuse change glides across a chunk
  like the other ramps.

**`dsp/echo.h`:** the send / return wrapper, the way Space wraps the Reverb.

```cpp
namespace af {
class Echo {
public:
    struct Params {
        Delay::Params delay;    // mix is forced to 1 (wet only); drive 0, glide Tape: not exposed in M2
    };
    Echo();                     // allocates the Delay's lines (UI thread)
    void reset();
    void set(const Params& p, const Transport& t);
    // sendL/R: the strata's summed Echo sends; out: the wet return (overwritten; it may be the
    // send's own buffers). Any n: the Delay runs in 32-sample chunks.
    void process(const float* sendL, const float* sendR, float* outL, float* outR, int n);
    bool silent() const;        // the repeats are under -120 dBFS and nothing is coming in
};
inline constexpr const char* kEchoModeNames[] = {"Stereo", "Ping-Pong", "Mono"};   // Delay::Mode
}
```

`silent()` works as Space's does: the send and the wet both under −120 dBFS for as long as a repeat
takes to come round (the longest time × repeats until −120 dB at the feedback, `tailSamples()`), the
counts saturating. With feedback 1 it is never silent while repeats sound.

**Echo's defaults** (Init's, though nothing is sent there), `initEcho()` in `echo.h`: Sync on at
"1/4." (the dotted quarter, 1.5 beats), free time 450 ms, feedback 0.45, low cut 150 Hz, high cut
4500 Hz, wow 0.3, duck 0.3, diffuse 0.3, mode Stereo, spread 0, drive 0, glide Tape.

**Checks:**
1. Every ported EffectForce Delay check passes.
2. **Diffuse 0** is bit for bit EffectForce's Delay over 10 s of noise at every mode.
3. **Diffuse smears:** an impulse at feedback 0.7, time 300 ms: with diffuse 1 the third repeat's
   energy is spread over at least 4 times as many samples above −40 dB of its peak as with diffuse 0,
   and its total energy is within ±1.5 dB.
4. **No growth:** feedback 1, diffuse 1, wow 1, every mode, a 1 s burst, then 120 s: the last
   second's peak is no higher than the peak at second 2.
5. **Echo is wet only:** an impulse in gives nothing at the output before the delay time.
6. **`silent()`:** true 0.5 s after the repeats of a 0.3 s burst at feedback 0.3 die under −120 dBFS;
   false while feedback 1 holds them.
7. **Cost:** `make arm-icount` isn't wired to Echo yet; count it with a module-level harness or leave
   the number to Task 8, and say which in the header.

**Commit:** "Echo: EffectForce's Delay with Diffuse, as a send / return".

## Task 3: Air's voices

**Files:** create `dsp/airvoices.h`, `dsp/airvoices.cpp`, `test/airvoices_test.cpp`
(`void airvoicesTests()`; `make test-module M=airvoices`).

**Interface:**

```cpp
namespace af {
enum AirSound : int { AS_GLASS, AS_BOWL, AS_BAR, AS_BELL, AS_KALIMBA, AS_FELT, AS_COUNT };
inline constexpr const char* kAirSoundNames[] = {"Glass", "Bowl", "Bar", "Bell", "Kalimba", "Felt"};

struct AirVoicePatch {
    int sound = AS_GLASS;
    float toneHz = 6000.0f;    // 200..16000: the strike's brightness (the modes' and the pluck's low-pass)
    float decayS = 4.0f;       // 0.1..20: the fundamental's ring to -60 dB
    float width = 0.7f;        // 0..1: how far apart the notes' pans may fall
};

class AirVoices {
public:
    static constexpr int kVoices = 6;     // the cap: 6 -> 4 if the device bench asks
    static constexpr int kModes = 6;
    AirVoices();
    void seed(uint32_t s);
    void set(const AirVoicePatch& p, const HarmonyPatch& h);   // h: the tuning (tunedPitch)
    // A note now (MIDI 24..108, tuned by h), vel 0..1, pan -1..1: a free voice, else the quietest
    // (a voice taken fades out over 2 ms before the new note starts in it).
    void strike(int note, float vel, float pan);
    void reset();
    // Adds the dry into outL/outR and the send at spaceSend into sendL/sendR. n <= 128.
    void render(const TableSet& tables, float* outL, float* outR, float* sendL, float* sendR,
                float spaceSend, int n);
    int active() const;        // voices ringing above -90 dBFS (or fading for a steal)
};
}
```

**Behaviour:**
- **Modal sounds** (Glass, Bowl, Bar, Bell): 6 modes per voice, each a decaying complex one-pole
  (`z = z × r e^{iω}`, the output its imaginary part), set going by the strike with mode k's amplitude
  `a_k × lp(f_k)` (`lp`: 12 dB an octave above Tone), its decay time `decayS / d_k`. Four modes to a
  vector (two vectors a voice), so 36 modes cost about 18 vector multiply-adds a sample. A mode above
  18 kHz is left out. The output rises over 1.5 ms (a raised cosine), so a strike never clicks.

  | Sound | Ratios f_k / f | Amplitudes a_k | Decay divisors d_k |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Glass | 1, 2.32, 4.25, 6.63, 9.38, 12.5 | 1, 0.6, 0.4, 0.25, 0.15, 0.1 | 1, 1.6, 2.4, 3.5, 4.8, 6 |
  | Bowl | 1, 1.004, 2.71, 2.717, 5.12, 8.18 | 1, 0.8, 0.5, 0.4, 0.25, 0.12 | 1, 1, 1.5, 1.5, 2.2, 3 |
  | Bar | 1, 2.756, 5.404, 8.933, 13.34, 18.64 | 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.12, 0.06, 0.03 | 1, 2, 3.5, 5, 7, 9 |
  | Bell | 0.5, 1, 1.183, 1.506, 2, 2.514 | 0.6, 1, 0.7, 0.5, 0.6, 0.3 | 0.6, 1, 1.4, 1.8, 2.2, 3 |

  Bowl's close pairs beat (1.004: 1 Hz at 262 Hz). The sum is scaled so a strike at vel 1 peaks
  near −6 dBFS before the level (each sound's own constant, set by measuring, stated in the header).
- **Kalimba:** a Karplus–Strong pluck: a delay line of the period (fractional, Lagrange or allpass
  tuned within ±2 cents), a one-pole low-pass in the loop at Tone, the loop gain from Decay
  (`g = 10^(-3 / (decayS × f))`), excited by a 2 ms burst of noise low-passed at Tone.
- **Felt:** the Felt Piano lifetime table (`TB_FELT_PIANO`) read by `TableOscLinear`, Age moving from
  0.05 to 0.8 over the decay (the note darkens as it fades), 5 ms attack, an exponential decay over
  `decayS`. The position moves once per control step, so most renders read a pair of frames.
- **Allocation:** a free voice; else the quietest (by its envelope now); the steal's 2 ms fade.
- **Pans:** each note at its given pan, equal power (unity in the middle).
- **Velocity:** gain `0.25 + 0.75 × vel` (a soft strike is still heard).
- **Sleeping:** a voice under −90 dBFS for 32 samples is free and isn't rendered. `active() == 0`
  costs nothing.

**Checks:**
1. **Pitch:** each sound's note 69 (Equal) has its fundamental (Bell: its prime, ratio 1) at 440 Hz
   within 1 cent (Kalimba within 2 cents), by peak interpolation on a long FFT.
2. **Inharmonic:** Glass's second mode at 2.32 × f within 0.5%.
3. **Decay:** the fundamental's level falls 60 dB in decayS ± 10% (Decay 2 s and 8 s, every modal
   sound and Kalimba).
4. **Tone:** Tone 500 Hz puts the band above 4 kHz at least 20 dB lower than Tone 16000 does.
5. **No click:** the largest sample-to-sample step in a strike's first 2 ms is no more than 3 times
   the largest step over its next 20 ms.
6. **Steal:** 7 strikes → `active() == 6`, the quietest one gone; no step > 0.25.
7. **Sleep:** after a strike rings out, `active() == 0` within decayS × 1.6, counted from the end of
   the strike's excitation (the modes' and Felt's: the strike; the Kalimba's: its burst, a period
   below note 72). *Amended 2026-10-08:* counted from the strike, it asked a voice to sleep sooner
   than the −90 dBFS rule lets it. At Decay 0.1 a low pluck's burst is a third of its ring, and the
   pluck itself stays above −90 dBFS until 1.607 Decays after the strike (note 25, a hard pan's
   louder side). The test holds it to 1.6 × Decay plus half the burst.
8. **Stability:** every sound at notes 24 and 108, Decay 20, Tone 16000, 6 voices re-struck every
   100 ms for 30 s: finite, peak ≤ 2.
9. **Cost** (`make test-module-arm M=airvoices` prints it, or Task 1's harness): 6 voices ringing,
   each sound; the dearest within 32k ARM instructions a block (header states the figures).

**Commit:** "Air's voices: a modal bank (Glass, Bowl, Bar, Bell), Kalimba and Felt".

## Task 4: Air's generator

Pure logic, so TDD first.

**Files:** create `dsp/airgen.h`, `dsp/airgen.cpp`, `test/airgen_test.cpp` (`void airGenTests()`;
`make test-module M=airgen`).

**Interface:**

```cpp
namespace af {
enum AirPattern : int { AP_RANDOM, AP_RISE, AP_FALL, AP_CONSTELLATION, AP_ECHO, AP_COUNT };
inline constexpr const char* kAirPatternNames[] = {"Random", "Rise", "Fall", "Constellation", "Echo"};

struct AirGenPatch {
    float density = 12.0f;      // events a minute, 0..60 (a Poisson process)
    int pattern = AP_CONSTELLATION;
    int registerOct = 5;        // the range starts at the key's tonic in this octave: 4, 5, 6 (C4 = 60)
    float rangeOct = 2.0f;      // 0.5..3: how far above it the notes go
    float gravity = 0.6f;       // 0..1: chord tones weigh 1, other scale tones 1 - gravity
    int motif = 5;              // 3..8: a Constellation's (and Echo's) notes
    float mutate = 0.3f;        // 0..1: the chance a pass swaps one note for a scale neighbour
    bool loop = false;
    float loopS = 16.0f;        // 2..120: the loop's length, free
    float loopBeats = 0.0f;     // synced: its length in quarter notes (common.h kBarDivBeats); 0: free
    float rubato = 0.2f;        // 0..1: jitter on velocity, and on a loop's timing
};

struct AirEvent {
    int offset;   // samples into the step()
    int note;     // MIDI
    float vel;    // 0..1
};

class AirGen {
public:
    static constexpr int kLoopMax = 64;   // events a loop keeps
    void seed(uint32_t s);
    void set(const AirGenPatch& p, const HarmonyPatch& h);
    // What it generates from: the chord (its pitch classes and root) and whether to generate at all
    // (Harmony with a chord, or Free: yes; Notes, or a forgotten harmony: no). A loop replays either way.
    void setChord(const Chord& c, bool generate);
    // A note the player gave Air (Notes, or a key above Split): Echo's motif learns it, a loop records it.
    void played(int note, float vel, int offset);
    void setTransport(double bpm, double beats, bool locked) { clock_.set(bpm, beats, locked); }
    void reset();
    // Moves on by n samples (n <= 128); writes the events due in them, in order, at most `max`; returns
    // how many. The same seed and the same calls give the same events.
    int step(int n, AirEvent* out, int max);
};
}
```

**Behaviour:**
- **Timing:** generated events form a Poisson process at `density` a minute: each gap exponential
  with mean 60 / density s, drawn when the event before plays. Density 0 generates nothing. A
  density change takes effect from the next gap.
- **Candidates:** the scale's tones (h.key, h.scale; Chromatic: all twelve) from the tonic in
  `registerOct` up to `rangeOct` octaves over it. A chord tone (its pitch class in the chord) weighs
  1, another scale tone `1 - gravity`. **The last two notes played are never chosen** (if that
  leaves nothing, only the last one; if still nothing, any).
- **Random:** each event a weighted draw.
- **Rise / Fall:** each event the candidate next above (below) the last, by weight among the next
  two; past the top (bottom) it starts again from the bottom (top) of the range.
- **Constellation:** a motif of `motif` notes drawn as Random does, built once, replayed in order one
  note per event. At the end of each pass, with probability `mutate`, one of its notes (at random) moves
  to a scale neighbour (the next scale tone up or down, in range) that keeps "no repeat within two"
  around it cyclically. When the key, scale or chord changes, each motif note not among the
  candidates moves to the nearest one (the lower on a tie).
- **Echo:** the motif is the last `motif` notes the player gave (`played()`), moved by octaves into
  the range, consecutive repeats dropped; replayed and mutated like a Constellation. Before the
  player has given any, it is a Constellation.
- **Velocity:** `0.7 × (1 - 0.5 × rubato × u)`, u uniform 0..1; played notes keep their own.
- **Loop** (CONCEPT §5.3, the Airports principle): on, the first pass (from when it was turned on,
  or the first event after `reset()`) records every event Air plays, generated or played, at its time
  in the pass, up to kLoopMax. From the second pass on the recording replays: each event at its time
  ± `rubato × 0.1 × gap` (gap: the time to its neighbour, so the order never changes), with the
  velocity jitter; nothing is generated; played notes are added to the recording (overdub). A key,
  scale or chord change moves each replayed note to the nearest candidate. Synced
  (`loopBeats > 0`), a pass is `loopBeats` quarter notes on the stratum's `BeatClock`, passes starting
  where the clock crosses a multiple of it (on the bar while MPC plays); a pass longer than 120 s at
  the tempo halves. Off: the recording is forgotten and generation goes on.
- **Determinism:** one xorshift, seeded by `seed()`; every draw happens whether or not its result is
  used, so a setting change never shifts the sequence that follows.

**Checks** (CONCEPT §13's `air` test):
1. **Same seed, same events** over 10 minutes; another seed, other events.
2. **Gravity 1:** 1000 Random events, every one a chord tone. **Gravity 0:** chord tones about as
   often as their share of the scale (within ±5 points).
3. **No repeats within two:** every pattern, 1000 events, no note equal to either of the two before
   (a chord of one tone with Gravity 1 and range 0.5: the fallbacks, still no note equal to the one
   before when two candidates exist).
4. **Density:** 10 minutes at 6, 12 and 60 a minute give the rate within ±10%.
5. **Range:** every note within tonic(registerOct) .. + 12 × rangeOct.
6. **Rise:** each note above the one before except at the wrap; **Fall** the mirror.
7. **Constellation:** with mutate 0 the same `motif` notes repeat in order; with mutate 1 each pass
   differs from the one before in exactly one note, by one scale step.
8. **Echo:** after `played()` of 62, 64, 67, the motif holds their pitch classes in range.
9. **Loop:** loopS 10 with density 30: from the second pass the events of each pass match the first
   pass's notes and times within the rubato jitter; synced at 120 BPM, 4 beats, passes start every 2 s
   on the beat.
10. **Notes off:** `setChord(c, false)` with no loop: no events.

**Commit:** "Air's generator: Poisson timing, Gravity, Rise, Fall, Constellation, Echo, Loop, Rubato".

// A Weather source: stereo, 16 bit, at three rates (EffectForce's levels: a grain pitched up reads a
// decimated copy, so what it would shift past 22 kHz was filtered out first). Interleaved L R. Each

struct GrainSource {

    static constexpr int kGuard = 256;

**Behaviour:**
- **The anchor:** a point in the source, `position` plus a smoothed random walk (Drift: a step every
  control step, its speed `drift²` × the source per 20 s, its wander bounded to ±0.5), wrapping.
- **Cloud:** EffectForce's Cloud: Hann grains of `sizeS` at intervals `sizeS / grains` jittered
  ±30%, each starting at the anchor ± spray scatter, at the grain's rate, panned at random within
  ±width, backwards with probability `reverse`. Level `1 / sqrt(0.375 × grains)`.
- **Stretch:** the anchor plus a head that crawls through the source at 1/8 of real time (8 times
  stretched); grains (overlap `grains`, intervals jittered ±15%) read just behind it, scattered
  5 + 60 × spray ms.
- **Stream:** the source as it is, drifting: two grains of `sizeS` (at least 0.2 s) at overlap 2,
  each reading on from where the read point has got to (the anchor plus the time since Stream began,
  at rate 1), so consecutive grains join seamlessly; spray and reverse don't apply; Pitch and To Key
  pitch it (a granular shift: the time runs at rate 1).
- **Pitch and To Key:** the grain's transposition t in semitones:
  - Off: `pitch` plus a detune of up to ±6 cents × spray (Cloud only).
  - Scale or Chord: a pitch class q drawn uniformly from the scale's pitch classes (Scale) or from
    `setChord()`'s (Chord; none: the scale's), then t = the transposition nearest to `pitch` (within
    −6..+5) with (0 + t) mod 12 = q: every source's root is C (decision 5). No detune.
  - Its rate is 2^(t/12) at level 0, or the decimated level EffectForce picks (a grain faster than
    1.26 reads level 1, faster than 2.2 level 2), so +24 never aliases.
- **The reads:** EffectForce's staged cubic reads, two samples a vector, converting the 16-bit
  samples as they load; a grain stays inside the source's loop (it wraps through the guard frames).
- **Tilt and HP** after the grains, in stereo: the output's tilt shelf (one first-order shelf at
  800 Hz, ±6 dB) and a one-pole high-pass (bypassed at 20 Hz). Coefficients per control step.
- **Duck:** an envelope of duckPeak per piece (50 ms attack, 1.5 s release), the gain
  `1 / (1 + 16 × duck × env)`, gliding across the piece.
- **Gate:** `gate(true)` fades in over kGateS (linear in dB from −60), `gate(false)` out; at −60 dB
  every grain stops and nothing is rendered. A source change, a mode change, or `reset()` fades the
  grains out over 20 ms (EffectForce's mode fade).
- **Level, mute, the send:** as Ground's: the level and mute glide (10 ms), the send is taken after
  level, gate and duck.

**`buildSource`** pads the frames to a multiple of 4 (the decimators halve twice), writes the guard
frames, and is deterministic.

**Checks:**
1. **buildSource:** a 1 kHz sine of 3 s comes out at −20 dBFS RMS ± 0.1 dB; its loop seam (the last
   frame to the first) has no step larger than the sine's own largest step × 1.5; level 1 at 500 Hz
   and level 2 at 250 Hz half-sample rates hold the sine; a 21 kHz sine is at least 80 dB down in
   level 1.
2. **Silence without a source:** `render(nullptr, …)` adds exact zeros.
3. **Pitch:** a sine source at 440 Hz, Cloud, spray 0, pitch +7, To Key Off → a 659.3 Hz peak within
   3 cents; pitch +24 → 1760 Hz, and nothing above −70 dB below 15 kHz but the tone and its
   window's sidebands (no aliasing: level 2's read).
4. **To Key Chord:** a C source (sine at 261.63 Hz), chord pcs {D, F#, A}: every grain's
   transposition mod 12 is 2, 6 or 9 (checked through a test hook that records each grain's t), and
   every one is within −6..+5 of pitch 0.
5. **Level:** white-noise source at −20 dBFS RMS, Cloud, grains 1, 4 and 16: the output RMS within
   ±1.5 dB of each other (the 1/sqrt(overlap) law).
6. **Stream joins:** a 220 Hz sine source, Stream, size 0.5 s: the output's amplitude envelope

7. **Gate:** after `gate(true)`, the level at kGateS / 2 is −30 dB ± 3 dB of steady; after

8. **Duck:** duck 1, duckPeak at −6 dBFS for 2 s: the output at least 12 dB down; 3 s after it stops,

  when it went out), or when none runs and the last `blockDone` said nothing is held; and at once when no
  block can have seen it (the counts before the store that put it in and after the one that took it out
  agree, none running). 64-bit counters. The rule is two pure functions (`graveMayFree`,
  `graveNeverSeen`) and `test/sources_test.cpp` runs them over every interleaving of a block's six steps
  with the worker's swaps and checks (about 950 000 states in a second on x86, 110 000 under qemu); PolyForce's rule and four
  weakened ones fail the same model. A swap is exception-safe (everything that can throw is done
  before the store to `live`).
- **`Loader`** besides PolyForce's: `post(job)` (a job on the worker, for Keep), `rounds()` (tests),
  `blockDone(holds)`, and an explicit pick (`want(slot, key, now = true)`) of the key already loaded
  loads it again: the source is swapped only if the load function returns another object (a
  `FileStamp` of size and time in `SourceCache` says whether the file changed), and a file that has
  gone makes the slot Missing. A scroll (`now = false`) over the loaded key does nothing. What a load
  function, a listener or a job captured is destroyed off the loader's lock.
- **`memory:` loads nothing** (`loadSource("memory:")` is nullptr with "Memory plays from the engine"), so
  a slot wanting it is Missing; Task 10 special-cases the key (it sets `weather.memory`).
- **The key grammar** (Task 10's `surface.py` preset check should accept the same): `builtin:<field
  name>` (one of `kFieldNames`), `memory:`, `plugin:Weather/<path>.wav`,
  `ssd:AmbientForce/Weather/<path>.wav`, `ssd:AmbientForce/Memories/<path>.wav`; the path may name
  subfolders (four levels), the extension is `.wav` in any case, nothing else is a key; the roots
  are `sourceRoots()`, `AF_SOURCE_ROOTS` replaces them.
- **WAVs:** rates 1000 to 384000 Hz, 16, 24 and 32-bit PCM and 32-bit float, 1 to 8 channels
  (EXTENSIBLE too); a data chunk longer than the file is a truncated file and fails (the sizes a
  streaming writer leaves, 0xFFFFFFFF and 0 with no chunk after, run to the end of the file); at most
  60 s are read, in blocks, so memory is bounded by the output. **The resampler as realised:** above 96 kHz
  the rate is first halved by `dsp/halfband.h`'s decimator (once for 192 and 176.4 kHz, twice for 384 kHz;
  its 3.19-frame delay made up), then a 32-tap Kaiser (beta 7) windowed sinc with 256 interpolated
  phases and a cutoff of 0.45 of the lower rate. Measured (gain at 15, 18, 19.845, 21 kHz): from 48 kHz
  0.00, -0.61, -6.02, -14.83 dB; from 88.2 and 176.4 kHz -0.10, -2.11, -6.02, -10.12; from 96, 192 and 384
  kHz -0.18, -2.32, -6.02, -9.72. Tones that would fold into the band are 73 dB down or more from 96 kHz,
  a 25 kHz tone from 192 or 384 kHz lands 37 dB down (it was at -16 and -9.9 dB through the taps alone),
  30 kHz and up 93 dB down or more. Files at 88.2 kHz and up are dulled from 15 kHz (the taps work at
  up to 96 kHz, where their transition is twice as wide in Hz as at 48 kHz).
- **Keep:** the file is the remembered ring from its oldest frame (`origin`), 16-bit stereo, at the
  level it was played: the ring holds the tap 6 dB down for headroom and the file has that back, clamped
  (the Strata tap is taken before the limiter, so a loud mix can clip in the file; a sine of 0.5 comes
  back at 0.5). **The number** is one past the highest ever used, never a deleted file's, as the user presets
  do (`.last` in the folder keeps the highest; the name is claimed with `O_EXCL`; numbers run to 999999, and a note or a name
  with a number over that is ignored; an empty file over a minute old, what a crash between the claim and the write
  leaves, is cleared by the next Keep and its number not used again): a project or preset
  that names a deleted Memory shows MISSING instead of playing other audio, and a cached source is
  dropped when its file changes or goes. **The pin** is held while the ring is copied into the file's
  buffer and let go before the write, so Remember is refused only for that copy (decision 9).
- **Additions:** `sourceOf(live)`, `rescanSources()`, `sourcesMade()` and `setKeepHook()` (stages
  `KS_COPY`, `KS_NUMBERED`, `KS_WRITE`) in `plugin/sources.h`; `Makefile`: `make test-module M=sources`
  builds the plugin files its suite needs (`MOD_PLUGIN_sources`) and `-pthread`. `readWav` also
  reports `fileRate`; its `rate` is always 44100.
- **For Tasks 8 and 10:** pass `blockDone(weather audible)`, hand Weather `live(0)` in every block in which it

**Routing** (CONCEPT §4.1), added to the M1 rules:
- **Air, Notes:** each key down plays its mapped note on Air (`play`). **Harmony:**
  `setChord(current(), current().root >= 0)` whenever the harmony's version changes. **Free:** the
  tonic chord from the first note on. Nothing before the first note.
- **Split:** a key at or above `split` goes to Air only (`play`, its mapped note), whatever Air's
  Listen: it doesn't enter the harmony, Bloom or Ground, and its note-off does nothing more. Keys
  under it are as before.
- **Weather:** **Notes**: gated on while any key is held (a finger, the pedal or Hold), To Key
  Chord on the held notes' pitch classes. **Harmony**: gated on while the harmony has a chord, its
  pitch classes. **Free**: on from the first note until Stop, the tonic chord's.
- **Weather's source:** `setWeatherSource(const GrainSource*)`, once a block before `render()` (the
  plugin's loader); with `weather.memory` the engine gives Weather `memory_.remembered()` instead.
- **Remember:** `void remember()`, called by the plugin before a block; applies at the next control
  step (`Memory::remember()`). `Info` reports `remembered`, `memoryFill`, `memoryGeneration`. The
  order in a piece is Remember (at the control step's start) → the strata (Weather renders, sees the
  source change and copies what its fading grains still read from the old ring) → Memory writes
  last: applied after Weather had rendered, the next `write()` could record over the old ring's
  first frames while Weather's grains still read them, and the copies taken at the change would
  capture overwritten audio (a click). *(Added in Task 8, from Task 6's review.)*

**The mix,** per piece: Ground, Bloom, Air and Weather render (each into its own buffer whenever its
Echo send or Memory needs its dry apart; centred and unsent, straight into the bus as now); Weather is
given Bloom's dry peak for Duck. The Echo bus is each stratum's dry (before its pan) times its Echo
send, gliding 10 ms; Echo runs on it (when any send is above 0, or `!silent()`); its return goes
into the mix × `echoReturn` and into the Space bus × `echoSpace`; then Space; then Memory records its
tap (Strata: the four strata's dry sum; Output: dry + Echo + Space returns, before tilt); then the
output as in M1. Sends to Echo and Space are 0 where their returns are 0, as Ground's Space send is.
